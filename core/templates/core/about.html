We believe every student deserves access to life-changing internships and scholarships. InternLink was born from that belief.

Our Mission

InternLink exists to bridge the gap between talented students and the opportunities that will define their careers. Too many students miss out simply because they don't know where to look or don't have the right connections.

We're changing that — one application at a time. By centralizing internships and scholarships in one platform, we make it easy for any student to discover, apply, and track their journey toward a brighter future.

From a first-year HND student to a final-year degree candidate — InternLink is for everyone.
